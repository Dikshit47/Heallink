# HEAL LINK — AI-Powered Medical Equipment Rental & Home-Care Platform

> **"From finding the equipment to completing the care journey."**  
> *Plan. Find. Verify. Rent. Deliver. Return.*

HEAL LINK is an AI-powered unified healthcare ecosystem that solves the fragmentation, trust, availability, affordability, and lifecycle-management challenges of medical equipment rental and home care.

---

## 🌟 Key USPs & Capabilities

1. **USP 1 — AI Care Journey Twin**: Enter natural language patient situations (e.g., *"My father had hip surgery and needs home recovery for 45 days"*). AI projects a multi-week care timeline, required equipment phases, clinical reminders, and rental milestones. Includes clinical non-diagnostic disclaimers.
2. **USP 2 — AI Equipment Bundle Generator**: Automatically groups relevant equipment (Hospital Bed, Walker, Commode Chair, Air Mattress, Wheelchair) with up to 25% bundled savings and explicit clinical rationales for every item.
3. **USP 3 — Auditable Equipment Trust Score**: Every unit carries an auditable rating (e.g., 96/100) broken down into Sanitization (100%), Functional Tests (98%), Maintenance (96%), Certification (100%), Age (90%), and Vendor Reliability (95%).
4. **USP 4 — QR Equipment Health Passport**: Scan or open digital serial credentials showing recent hospital disinfection, 14-point functional calibration, maintenance logs, condition grades, and prior rental history.
5. **USP 5 — AI Emergency Equipment Swarm**: Dedicated high-urgency mode locating verified equipment across nearby vendor pools ranked by road transit ETA (e.g. 20-30 min) with one-click emergency dispatch.
6. **USP 6 — AI Rent vs Buy vs Rent-to-Own Calculator**: Interactive duration slider comparing deterministic financial totals for renting, outright buying, and rent-to-own with AI financial rationales.
7. **USP 7 & 16 — AI Return & Reuse Sanitization Protocol**: 7-stage biomedical pipeline (Returned → Inspection → Deep Cleaning → Disinfection → Functional Test → Technician Sign-off → Ready for Next Rental).
8. **USP 8 & 11 — Multi-Vendor Marketplace & Comparison**: Compare 2 to 4 equipment units side-by-side with automatic badges (*Best Value*, *Fastest ETA*, *Highest Trust*).
9. **USP 9 & 17 — 100% Transparent Pricing**: Zero "Call for Price" and zero "₹X" placeholders. Shows Daily, Weekly, Monthly rates, itemized delivery, technician installation, pickup, and separated 100% refundable security deposit.
10. **USP 10 & 19 — Family Caregiver Dashboard**: Track active home patient recovery (Rajesh Kumar - Hip Surgery), countdown timers, scheduled physiotherapist/nurse home visits, and one-click renewals/returns.

## 🚀 How to Run the Application

### Method 1: Desktop One-Click Launcher (Recommended)
Double-click the **`Start_HealLink.bat`** file located on your **Desktop**.
It will launch the FastAPI backend server and automatically open your default browser to `http://localhost:8000`.

### Method 2: Command Line
Open PowerShell or Command Prompt:
```bash
cd C:\Users\diksh\Desktop\HealLink
python app.py
```
Then visit: [http://localhost:8000](http://localhost:8000)

## 🔒 Medical & Clinical Safety
HEAL LINK is designed as an equipment logistics, planning, and transparency platform. It does **not** diagnose medical conditions or prescribe drugs. All recommendations feature prominent medical advisory disclaimers advising patients and families to consult treating physicians.
